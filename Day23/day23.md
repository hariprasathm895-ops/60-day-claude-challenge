## Ideal Customer Profile
- **Primary:** Engineering/technical college student, 1st–3rd year, at a
  mid-tier private technical campus in India
- **Secondary:** Club lead, course rep, or faculty coordinator who gates
  adoption

## Top 10 Customer Pain Points (highlights)
1. Coursework scattered across 3–4 disconnected apps
2. No single shared deadline tracker
3. Generic quiz tools not tailored to a specific course/club
4. No lightweight way to confirm task completion
5. Low motivation — no gamification or visible progress
... full list of 10 in the PDF

## MVP Recommendation
- **Build first:** One unified hub for a single pilot club/course — file
  sharing, shared deadline tracker, one simple quiz tool. Nothing more.
- **Do NOT build yet:** Gamification, payments, multi-institution admin
  dashboard, native mobile app.
- **Success metrics:** ≥60% pilot login in week 1, ≥1 upload/task per
  active member per week, retained into week 3–4 without founder
  prompting, satisfaction ≥7.5/10 (current baseline).

## MoSCoW Prioritization
| Priority | Features |
|---|---|
| Must Have | Unified file hub, shared deadline tracker, basic login/roles, one quiz tool |
| Should Have | Notifications/reminders, lightweight activity view for coordinator |
| Could Have | Gamification (badges/points), discussion threads |
| Won't Have (now) | Payments, multi-college admin panel, native mobile app, AI personalization |

## Pricing Hypothesis
Stay free for individual students/clubs; test monetization at the
department/institution level (paid admin tier), since students are highly
price-sensitive and unlikely to pay individually.

## Top 5 Risks
Technical/scaling (High) · Execution bandwidth (High) · IP/ownership with
SIH teammates (Medium) · Distribution (Medium) · Monetization (Medium) —
consistent with Day 22's risk assessment.

## 30-Day MVP Plan
| Week | Focus | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stabilize & scope | Lock MVP to Must-Have only; fix top backend bugs |
| 2 | Pilot launch | Onboard one real club/course; track activation live |
| 3 | Iterate | Fix the friction points the pilot group actually hits |
| 4 | Decide | Review retention data; decide on a 2nd pilot |
